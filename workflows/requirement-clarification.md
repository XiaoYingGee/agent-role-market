# Requirement Clarification Workflow

## Use When

- The request is ambiguous.
- Scope, priority, or acceptance criteria are unclear.
- Multiple teams or roles may be involved.

## Roles

- Primary: `product-manager`
- Supporting: `business-analyst`, `technical-writer`
- Coordinator: `task-coordinator` when multiple follow-up tasks are needed

## Phases

### 1. Intake

Capture the request, requester goal, constraints, and known context.

Output: initial task card.

### 2. Clarify

Define goals, non-goals, users, business rules, and open questions.

Output: requirement summary.

### 3. Acceptance

Turn the requirement into testable acceptance criteria.

Output: acceptance checklist.

### 4. Split

Break the work into design, engineering, QA, or documentation tasks.

Output: task breakdown with owners and recommended role packs.

## Required Handoff

- Problem statement
- Goals and non-goals
- Acceptance criteria
- Open decisions
- Suggested next workflow

## Completion Criteria

The next role can proceed without asking what the task means.
